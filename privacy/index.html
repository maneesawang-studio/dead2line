Your privacy

Privacy policy

This policy explains what information Deadline handles, why it is used, which service providers help operate the app, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Your data

Your email is used for your account. Task titles, details, categories, deadlines, follow-up dates, and subtasks are stored in Supabase and linked to your account. Theme and language preferences are saved in your browser.

Storage and access

Supabase provides sign-in and task storage. Database policies are designed to limit task access to the signed-in account. Support messages, including your reply email, topic, and message, are sent through Resend for email delivery. The site also requests fonts from Google Fonts.

Managing your data

You can edit or permanently delete tasks from your dashboard. Signing out won't delete them. Account deletion isn't available in settings; use the contact form to request it.

Contact support

Important limitations

The app doesn't currently offer task export or restore, and it doesn't send reminders. Keep a separate copy of important information and use another reminder for critical deadlines.

How information is used

Information is used to create and maintain your account, save and display your tasks across sessions, apply your language and theme choices, and respond when you contact support. Deadline does not need the contents of your tasks to send notifications; the app currently sends no task reminders.

Browser storage and preferences

Deadline saves your theme and language preferences in your browser so they can be remembered on your next visit. The sign-in provider may also store session information in browser storage to keep you signed in. Clearing browser storage may reset preferences or sign you out, but it does not delete tasks already saved to your account.

Support messages

If you use the contact form, your reply email, selected topic, and message are sent to our support email through Resend so we can respond. Please include only the information needed to resolve your request. Support correspondence is handled in email and is not stored as a task.

When information is shared

Your tasks are not displayed to other users. Information is shared with the providers described above only as needed to run the service and handle support, or when disclosure is required by applicable law. Deadline does not sell task content.

How long information is kept

Tasks remain associated with your account until you delete them or request account deletion. Support messages may remain in support email records for as long as needed to handle your request and maintain relevant records. Service providers may retain limited copies in backups or logs according to their own retention practices.

Protecting your information

Deadline uses account-based access controls for task records, but no online service can promise absolute security. Use a unique password, sign out on shared devices, and avoid storing sensitive information in task descriptions or notes.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Deadline or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

Questions or data requests

For privacy questions or to request account deletion, contact support using the form below. We may ask for information needed to verify and respond to your request.
