TAKE DEADLINE WITH YOU

Use Deadline like an app

Add Deadline to your home screen or computer. It opens in its own app window, and your tasks stay synced to your account.

Install Deadline

On supported browsers, install directly from here. Otherwise, follow the steps for your device.

01

Android (Chrome)

Open this page in Chrome, tap the three-dot menu, then choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

02

iPhone or iPad (Safari)

Open this page in Safari, tap Share, choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.

03

Computer (Chrome or Edge)

Select Install app in the address bar or open the browser menu and choose Install Deadline.

04

Other browsers

Open the browser's Share or menu option and look for Add to Home Screen or Install app.

Your tasks stay in sync

The app opens in its own window, and your account keeps your data in sync. An internet connection is needed to load and update tasks.
