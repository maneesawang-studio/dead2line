Frequently asked questions

How do I add a task?

On the Tasks page, select + Add, then enter a task title and deadline. Category, time, notes, and subtasks are optional.

How do I mark a task as finished?

Select the checkmark on a task card. The task will then appear under Finished.

What does the red badge mean?

It marks an in-progress task that's due within three days or already overdue.

How do I change the theme or language?

Select the gear icon on the Tasks page to open the settings menu, then choose a theme or language.

How do I sign in?

Sign in with your Deadline account email and password. If you don't have an account, select Sign up.

Can other users see my tasks?

No. Your tasks are saved to your account and kept separate from other users' tasks.

What if I forget my password?

On the sign-in page, enter your email and select Forgot password? Use the link in your email to choose a new password. If it expires, request another link.

Will Deadline remind me about upcoming tasks?

Deadline doesn't send push or email reminders yet. Open your task list to check upcoming and overdue work.

Can I use Deadline without internet?

No. Installing Deadline adds an app-like shortcut, but you need an internet connection to load or update your tasks.

Can I export or restore my tasks?

The app doesn't currently provide a user-facing export or restore option. Keep a separate copy of critical information.
